## Description (copy everything between the lines)

---
AI can fill in forms, but can you trust the answers? TraceForm fills forms from messy documents with Meta's Muse Spark model, and every answer comes with proof.

▶ Try it: https://traceform-five.vercel.app/
▶ Code: https://github.com/Nana-Kojo801/traceform

HOW IT WORKS
• Meta's Muse Spark (muse-spark-1.3, Meta Model API) reads your emails, letters, Word files and photos, and answers each form field with a word-for-word quote.
• Code, not the AI, checks that every quote really exists in the source document.
• If a quote can't be found, Meta's model gets one repair pass: re-cite it word for word, or give up.
• A second Meta AI call adds an advisory second opinion.
• When documents disagree, TraceForm shows both sides. You choose.
• If no document mentions something, the field stays blank. No guessing.
• Photos and scans are read by Muse Spark vision, and you can compare the text with the original.
• You approve every answer, then export to CSV or JSON.

RESULTS (measured, small sample)
• 5 live runs on the sample documents: 45/45 fields correct, 0 invented values
• The conflict was flagged 5/5 times; the missing field was left blank 5/5 times
• Live repair tests: 3/3 fake citations caught
